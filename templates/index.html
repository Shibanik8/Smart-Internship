About the Project

The Smart Internship Management System (SIMS) is an enterprise-ready MVC application facilitating recruitment processes for students and business entities.

By embedding intelligent modules like UiPath RPA triggers, Snowflake data models, and analytical tools, SIMS scales easily from local university settings to cross-national corporate internship networks.
